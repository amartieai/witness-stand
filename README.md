# The Witness Stand

A judge for the judges.

Nobody should be able to ban you, remove you, or silence you without being certified first. The Witness Stand is a free, open gate that grades moderation decisions in the open, on four questions:

1. Was the material actually read?
2. Was the exact rule cited and quoted?
3. Was every claim verified against the material?
4. Did a named human being stamp it?

The poster carries the same badge: a post that plays dirty gets graded by the same gate. Nobody sits above the scales. The gate reads records, not stories.

## Live today

- The stand: https://www.reddit.com/r/TheWitnessStand/
- The public scorecard (12 decisions graded: 1 pass, 11 fails): https://www.reddit.com/r/TheWitnessStand/wiki/scorecard/
- Bring any removal or ban to the stand — it gets witnessed, graded, and put on the scorecard.

## The kit

The full downloadable kit — the gate pack for moderators, the AutoMod blocks, the templates, the witness-pair recorder, the scorecard tablet, and the certification sheet — lands here as the v1.0 release. Communities: request your audit at the stand, and your mods run through the gate first. A post that passes carries the stamp: *This post has been certified and ran through the witness stand.*

The full kit is published in this repository: [kit](https://github.com/amartieai/witness-stand/tree/main/kit) — and this repo is a template: press **Use this template** to grow your own stand.

## The demand

No certification, no ban hammer. Certification is revocable by the record, and the registry is public.
