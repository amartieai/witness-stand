# CERTIFICATION — how a community requests an audit, what certified means

Adoption runs through certification, one way or another.

## The demand

Certification is not only an offer. The stand's demand, in the founder's words:

> **No certification, no ban hammer.**

A moderator who will not run their own decisions through the gate asks for a trust they have
not earned. Nothing in law compels certification — the public record is the leverage, and it
does not expire.

## How a community requests an audit

1. The community installs the kit (MARTY_FOR_MODERATORS.md §3–§7 in the wiki, §8 pinned) and
   runs the ten fixtures in §7, publishing the pass/fail table.
2. The community requests its audit at the stand, submitting its wiki page, its scorecard, and a
   sample of past rulings.
3. The stand runs the community's moderators through the system first — their own decisions run
   through the four legs (a sample of past rulings, and new rulings pre-cleared under the
   prime-time rule).

## What certified means

A community whose moderators pass is entered in the public CERTIFIED registry at the stand, with
its scorecard. Certified means the gate has been applied to the community's own decisions and
held. It is a badge of being judged, not a seal of approval.

## The stamp

A decision that has passed the gate carries the line, in Frank's example words:

> "This post has been certified and ran through the witness stand"

The legs are shown beneath it (read · rule cited · claims verified · signed) and the frozen record
is linked. The stamp names what actually happened and links the proof; it is the gate's fourth
leg (STAMP) made visible to the public.

## The registry rule — amend, never delete

Certification is revocable by the record: a later witnessed failure amends the entry. The
registry is never deleted, only amended. An entry that is wrong gets a correction line, not an
erasure. This is the same rule as the mod note: corrected, not deleted.

## The flow, stated plainly

The kit goes out per community → the community requests its audit → its mods are run through the
system first → certified rulings carry the stamp. A community asking to be certified is a
community volunteering to be judged — the Moderator's Visit with a badge on it.

## Honest limit

The stand does not bind Reddit. Certification governs the moderator's own hand only. The stand
makes overrides visible rather than letting anyone guess who removed their post.
