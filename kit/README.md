# MARTY — WITNESS STAND KIT v1

Marty is a pre-action gate for every removal, ban and refusal on Reddit. It is four checks that
must pass, in order, before an enforcement action takes effect: Guard, Check, Judge, Stamp.

This kit is the adoptable package. It is given away free, under the MIT License, with no monetization,
no licensing, and no malice. It was invented for all mankind.

## Contents

- `MARTY_FOR_MODERATORS.md` — the policy pack, with the audit's corrections applied. Adopt by
  copying §3–§7 into your subreddit's wiki and pinning §8.
- `THE_FOUR_QUESTIONS.md` — the gate on one page.
- `automod/` — two AutoMod blocks, exactly as corrected, named for what they do.
- `templates/` — the ban template and saved responses from the pack, as standalone files.
- `recorder/` — minimal witness-pair recorder (stdlib Python only).
- `SCORECARD_TEMPLATE.md` — empty tablet in the stand's published scorecard format.
- `CERTIFICATION.md` — how a community requests an audit, what certified means, and the stamp.

## Honest limit

Marty does not bind Reddit. A sitewide spam filter, an admin action, or a subreddit-setting
change can override any moderator decision, including an approval. Marty governs the moderator's
own hand only. The stand makes overrides visible rather than letting anyone guess who removed
their post.
