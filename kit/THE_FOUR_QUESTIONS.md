# THE FOUR QUESTIONS — the gate on one page

Before any enforcement action takes effect, all four must pass, in order. Tie goes to the post.

## 1. Guard — can you read it?

The moderator must be able to read what they are acting on, and the rule must exist and apply.
If you cannot read it, you cannot remove it. You may report it, hold it, or ask. A removal of
something you have not seen is not moderation; it is a guess with a timestamp.

Legibility includes: a post you can open, a rule that exists, a crosspost whose original is
still present. If any of these fail, refuse at GATE 1.

## 2. Check — did you quote it, and is every claim verified or struck?

Every removal reason quotes the rule, word for word. Every factual claim in it is either
verified against the post or struck. We do not call your post "spam" or "low effort" as a
substitute for a rule.

Three attestations:
- (a) read — the moderator opened the post.
- (b) cited — the rule is quoted verbatim.
- (c) checked — every factual claim is verified or struck.

## 3. Judge — is a named human deciding anything that turns on judgement?

Anything that turns on a judgement about the poster is decided by a named human, never by a bot.
Bots hold; humans remove. Judgement rules may never be removed by machine.

## 4. Stamp — is it signed and appealable?

Every action is signed with the rule, what was read, who stamped it, and how to appeal. The stamp
outlives the 90-day mod log. Prefix every Marty note with `MARTY|v1|` so the weekly audit is one
search.

## Tie rule

Tie goes to the post. If a gate is ambiguous, the post stays.

## Prime-time rule

The gate runs before the removal, not after. A removal that fails the gate is refused, not
corrected later.

## The Moderator's Visit

Before ruling, the moderator visits the stand — the four questions, the scorecard, and the
record of how decisions get graded. Nobody stamps a decision who has not first stood where
the judged stand.

The moderator who acts is the moderator who is named. Nobody confirms a removal someone else
already made.

## Your appeal

Reply to the removal message. A human answers within 48 hours with *upheld*, *reversed*, or
*escalated*. We publish a monthly count of appeals, reversals, and stamps.
