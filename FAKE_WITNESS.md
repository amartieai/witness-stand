# FAKE WITNESS — spec v0.1 (Frank's dictation, Nov 1, 2026)

Status: DESIGN, from Frank's words. Announced publicly Nov 1, 2026
(r/TheWitnessStand) as an open community build — the method is proven on
the Stand; the instrument itself is new and NOT yet built. Family: a
sibling instrument of The Witness Stand — same gate, new target.
The Stand grades MODERATORS' decisions. Fake Witness grades SPEAKERS' claims.

## The problem, in Frank's words

Propaganda and fake AI content are everywhere — Facebook, X, the news, the
whole internet. Anybody can state anything, nobody verifies it, and the
person who did verify gets ignored. "Where is the true sustenance in it?
Is there real at all?"

## The instrument

Put any speaker — a person, an entity, an account, a news outlet — to THE
CHALLENGE: what they state is held against the actual record and verified.
They are also scored AGAINST THEMSELVES: their claims today against their
claims yesterday, their words against their own record.

The challenge runs through the same Gate as everything in this family:
1. The material was actually read (the whole statement, in context).
2. The exact claim is cited and quoted — no paraphrase verdicts.
3. Every claim is verified against the record, or struck.
4. A named human stamps the grade.

Plus the AI leg, asked on every item: Is this AI-generated? Is that
disclosed? Is it sourced — where is the proof under it, or is it bare
assertion wearing a confident voice?

## The chain method (how a grade is earned — Frank, Nov 1)

You don't grade a speaker on one post. You cross-verify the body of what
they have ALREADY put out:

1. Inventory their posts and every link they posted. It's all still there —
   the record persists whether they remember posting it or not.
2. Follow each link down its chain to the root: what was this actually
   about, at the source — not what the post said it was about.
3. Grade each claim against its root. Say vs. source.
4. Categorize by the PATTERN — what they repeatedly say, and what they
   repeatedly do. One miss is an error; the same miss forty times is a
   function.

This is the full-chain audit turned on speakers: every link followed to
the end of its branch, nothing graded from a headline or a screenshot of
a screenshot.

## The three tiers (Frank's scale)

- **FAKE** — the record shows their claims fail the gate, and it repeats.
  Their function is the spreading of confusion. The label is earned by the
  record, never by dislike.
- **NORMAL** — new, curious, unproven. The starting tier for everybody.
  Not a failing grade — a question not yet answered either way.
- **TRUE** — they state it AND back it: the research is done, the proof is
  shown, more homework than anybody else bothered to do (Frank's example:
  WikiLeaks). Claims survive the gate because the receipts are attached.

## The label rides the name

The grade lives in a public registry beside the name, the way the Stand's
Registry works: amend, never delete; a source is listed only after the
evidence is shown and the act repeated; the grade is revocable BY THE
RECORD — a FAKE who starts backing claims earns their way up, a TRUE who
starts inventing earns their way down. Tiers move on evidence only. When
that name posts again, anywhere, the label is already known.

## The guards (standing doctrine, applies unchanged)

- RECORDS, NOT STORIES. No label from a vibe, a pile-on, or a moderator's
  feelings. The gate is not moved by anybody's story.
- THE TIE GOES TO THE SPEAKER. A challenge that doesn't land solid stays
  NORMAL — never FAKE on a tie or a maybe.
- BADGE SYMMETRY. The challenger is graded by the same gate. A false or
  sloppy challenge is itself scored and shows on the challenger's record.
- The community runs the test. No central authority decides who is fake;
  the record decides, in the open, and anyone can re-run the check.

## The feed gate (the endgame, Frank's words)

"Maybe it should be gated before it even reaches your feed." The reader-
side mirror of Pre-Flight: a home-side filter (local, MIT, no phoning
home — same law as the rest of the kit) that reads the scorecard before
your feed does. TRUE passes clean. NORMAL passes marked *unproven*.
FAKE arrives flagged — or held, at the reader's own setting. You watch
what passed a witness, not whatever the flood carries in.

The economics are the enforcement (Frank's point): propaganda runs on
reach, and reach runs on ads. Gated content is unseen content; unseen
content earns nothing. The label never has to win an argument — it only
has to stand between the claim and the feed, and the business model of
lying starves on its own.

## Build path (proposed, Frank's gate first)

1. This spec → Frank's gate.
2. A speaker scorecard beside the existing per-community and per-moderator
   scorecards (same columns: claims tested, passed, failed, struck).
3. The registry extension (speaker entries, same amend-never-delete law).
4. A Fake Witness branch in the kit — per-platform claim checks, the same
   way the Stand grows branches.
5. The feed gate last — it is only as good as the scorecard behind it.

## Naming (Frank's ruling, Nov 1)

- The tiers stay FAKE / NORMAL / TRUE.
- For the social feed — is this contributor or commenter on YOUR feed
  fake or not — the title is FAKE WITNESS. For news in the feed, NEWS
  WITNESS. (TRUTH WITNESS was also spoken; the TRUE tier carries it.)
- Frank does not have to build it all himself, and the names are not his
  to dictate: the kit is free, the engine and the method are the same,
  and each community names its own branch for its own use — the way the
  Stand grows branches. What a community builds with it, and what they
  call it, is up to them.
